Little Hero

A short animated film celebrating twenty years of Baal Dan Charities, and the idea that bravery and compassion come in many forms.

Watch on our YouTube channel.

The story

Small acts of kindness can change a child's life.

Created by Sol Landerman (who also provides the narration), Little Hero was made in celebration of Baal Dan's twenty-year milestone. It follows a character who shows that bravery and compassion come in many forms.

Founder Tanya Pinto created the film to bring Baal Dan's core belief to life for young audiences, and encourages supporters to share it widely, and to support our mission of helping children in need.
