Giving FAQ

How is my donation used?

Your donation supports orphanages, food and education programs, and can also sponsor a specific child's education. Because Baal Dan is entirely volunteer-run, nearly 100% of what you give goes directly to feeding and educating children in need.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Yes. Baal Dan Charities is a 501(c)(3) public charity (Federal Tax ID 20-4658983). You'll receive a formal receipt for any gift over $250.

Can I give monthly instead of one-time?

Absolutely. We encourage it. Monthly gifts give our grantee partners the stability to plan food and education programs year-round. Toggle "Monthly" above to get started.

Does my employer match donations?

Many companies match employee gifts, including Microsoft (via MSGive), AT&T, Esurance, Wells Fargo, Tellabs, ING, Liberty Mutual and eBay. Use the search box above or check with your HR department.

Can I donate by check or wire instead of online?

Yes. Checks and money orders can be made payable to "Baal Dan Charities," and bank wires are available for larger gifts. Contact us for details.
