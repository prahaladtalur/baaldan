Where does your money go? The Nepal Audit

David Grisanti conducted an independent verification visit to our grantee organizations in Nepal, confirming that funds were properly allocated and put to work, a milestone that also marked Baal Dan's 21st anniversary.

It's part of a broader philosophy: as humanitarian expert David Miliband has noted, effective aid depends on simplified, targeted interventions (like malnutrition treatment) and transparent impact reporting. That's exactly the model Baal Dan follows across all four of our primary countries of operation.
