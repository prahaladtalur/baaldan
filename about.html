About Baal Dan Charities

Formed in 2005 to support the basic needs and social welfare of impoverished and vulnerable children, and run entirely by volunteers ever since.

Who we are

A public charity built on personal engagement

Baal Dan Charities USA is a 501(c)(3) US-registered public charity (Federal EIN 20-4658983), founded in 2005. We exist to support orphans, street children, children with special needs, refugees, and survivors of abuse and violence, anywhere the need is greatest.

Each year we distribute annual grants of $5,000 to $50,000 for food, supplies and education to vetted grassroots partners. Since our founding we've supported more than 14,000 children across 14 countries, and we currently assist over 1,000 children annually while feeding more than 1,000 children daily through funded programs.

Because our team is entirely volunteer, nearly 100% of every donation reaches the children we serve: our operating costs were just 1.1% in 2024, and have stayed under 2.5% every year since 2006.

How we work

Our Grassroots Approach

We believe local knowledge and cultural fluency are mission critical, and that personal, direct engagement is the only path to lasting change.

Direct impact, locally led

We work where larger NGOs often can't, in corrupt or food-insecure environments, by deploying aid directly through the communities that know them best.

A micro-grant model

Smaller, strategic grants (instead of large lump-sum transfers) reduce waste and corruption risk, so beneficiaries feel the impact immediately.

Needs-based & audited

Field staff identify, vet, research and audit local needs before any grant is made, and we independently verify that funds were properly used.

Our philosophy rests on Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs: shelter, safety, food, water, medical care and education are essential before a child can break the cycle of poverty and thrive.

Founder & President

Tanya Pinto

Tanya founded Baal Dan Charities in 2005, inspired by her grandfather Ramesh Gupta, an orphan who received an education and went on to win India's prestigious Filmfare Award for filmmaking. That same year, she took a sabbatical from advertising to volunteer at Mother Teresa's orphanage in Calcutta, an experience that reshaped her life's direction.

Under her leadership, Baal Dan has raised nearly $2 million, aided more than 14,000 children across 14 developing countries, built a school serving 150 children, supported 20+ orphanages, and helped deliver daily food to over 1,000 children.

Tanya currently serves as Principal UX Researcher at Microsoft (since 2017), focused on AI and Copilot. She previously ran Shakti Consulting LLC (2012–2017) in brand strategy and cause marketing, spent over a decade in brand management at The Richards Group (1999–2012), and consulted for World Vision International on global rebranding and field reporting.

Born in Mumbai and raised across Dubai and Perth before moving to the U.S. at 21, Tanya holds a B.A. in Mass Communications with First Class Honors from Curtin University, and dual citizenship in Australia and the United States. She has traveled to over 50 countries and worked in more than 20 of them.
